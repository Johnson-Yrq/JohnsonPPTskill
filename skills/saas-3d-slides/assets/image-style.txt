FORM AND MATERIALS
Use precise architectural and industrial-design forms with small controlled corner radii, crisp fine bevels and realistic thin edges. Large structural surfaces are warm-white satin porcelain or fine mineral composite. Use deep navy for focal display surfaces and selected architectural volumes. Use muted teal mainly in translucent glass, narrow inlays and restrained interface accents. Use champagne-gold metal sparingly as fine edging, joints and selected small focal details. Clay-like softness belongs in the surface shading rather than swollen geometry. Glass has visible clear edges, subtle refraction and restrained reflections; preserve visibility through it.
